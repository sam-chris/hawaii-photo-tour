Diamond Head

Diamond Head is a famous volcanic crater located near Honolulu. Its hiking trail leads visitors toward incredible views of the Pacific Ocean and the surrounding city. The crater is one of Oahu's most recognizable landmarks and a favorite destination for outdoor adventures.
