Hanauma Bay

Hanauma Bay is a beautiful marine nature preserve on Oahu's southeastern coast. The curved bay is surrounded by green hills and is known for its clear water and colorful marine life. It is a wonderful place to learn about Hawaii's ocean environment and the importance of protecting coral reefs.
