North Shore

Oahu's North Shore is famous for its powerful winter surf, laid-back communities, and beautiful beaches. Surfers from around the world visit this coastline to experience its impressive waves. During calmer conditions, the area is also a great place to enjoy the scenery and watch the ocean.
