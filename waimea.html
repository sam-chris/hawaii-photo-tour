Waimea Falls

Waimea Falls is a scenic waterfall surrounded by lush tropical plants in the Waimea Valley on Oahu's North Shore. The valley has beautiful gardens and walking paths that allow visitors to experience Hawaii's natural landscape. It offers a peaceful change from the island's busy beaches.
